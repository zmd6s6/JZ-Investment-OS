## Objective

Deliver the safe research-and-deliberation slice. Runtime roles must communicate through a
versioned, strict protocol behind `LLMGatewayPort`; all model and external text remains untrusted
until schema and domain validation. Committee debate is deterministically bounded to two rounds,
preserves explicit dissent and failure, and cannot create a Decision, approval, execution, or order.

## Deliverables

- [ ] `LLMGatewayPort`, provider-neutral request/response DTOs, bounded timeout/cost metadata,
  and a synthetic adapter for tests only
- [ ] Versioned strict `AgentOpinion` schema with evidence-backed factual observations, explicit
  assumptions/unknowns, role authority checks, bounded repair, and fail-closed outcomes
- [ ] Immutable, frozen `AnalysisContext` with stable input snapshot hashing and available-at
  Evidence visibility
- [ ] Role registry with fixed tool allowlists and prompt-bundle version/hash metadata
- [ ] Round-1 independent role execution, deterministic conflict detection, targeted round 2, and
  mandatory Devil's Advocate participation with an absolute two-round limit
- [ ] Append-only committee/session/message and Agent-run observability, including correlation ID,
  latency, bounded retries, usage, and sanitized output references
- [ ] Synthetic unit, property, integration, contract, and S1/S2/S8/S9/S15 regression fixtures
- [ ] Data lineage, status, and verification evidence

## Acceptance criteria

1. No free-form model text becomes an AgentOpinion: schema-invalid output permits at most two
   explicit repairs, then fails as `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` with no fabricated opinion.
2. Every factual observation references one or more Evidence IDs visible in the frozen context;
   unknowns and assumptions are structurally distinct and cannot masquerade as facts.
3. Role registration, prompt bundle hash, model request metadata, usage, latency, and sanitized
   raw-output reference are preserved for audit without recording secrets.
4. Round 1 executes the required independent roles against one immutable AnalysisContext; conflict
   detection is deterministic, Round 2 is targeted, includes Devil's Advocate, and no session can
   exceed two rounds.
5. Timeout, gateway failure, provider schema drift, invalid repair, and missing Evidence are
   explicit outcomes; they cannot be silently replaced by prose or high-confidence analysis.
6. Prompt-injection-like Evidence is quoted only as untrusted data and cannot alter role,
   allowlist, prompt version, policy, risk, approval, or execution behavior.
7. This stage creates neither a CIO final Decision nor an approval, execution, brokerage, or
   live-trading path. All fixtures are synthetic.

## Out of scope

- Portfolio fit, Risk assessment/Veto evaluation, deterministic sizing, CIO Decision, approval,
  execution, scheduler cadence, UI, Learning, real provider credentials, and live trading;
- altering investment limits, Risk Veto semantics, approval gates, or accepted ADRs.

## Risks and human decisions

No real provider is authorized for this stage. The implementation will use synthetic adapters and
will not choose a provider, model, production prompt, budget, data-retention policy, or investment
governance parameter. Those choices require human authorization before any non-synthetic use.
