## Objective

Deliver the personal daily operating loop: explicit-market-calendar scheduled work, durable replay
and failure visibility, human-readable reports, and four read-only personal UI views without
creating a live execution path.

## Deliverables

- [x] Daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly job definitions with explicit market timezone/calendar
- [ ] Durable idempotency, advisory locking, as-of replay, bounded recovery, and visible TaskRuns
- [x] Synthetic daily, weekly, and monthly reports that label facts, calculations, judgments,
  assumptions, and human decisions
- [ ] React/TypeScript `web/` workspace and the Portfolio, Opportunities, Watchlist, and Decision
  Journal pages
- [ ] Prominent simulation, data-as-of, evidence-freshness, Risk Veto, pending-approval, and
  operational-exception indicators
- [ ] Normal, failure, and boundary coverage for S12 and each user-visible scheduler/UI safety state

## Acceptance criteria

1. Jobs never use server-local time as business time; each has explicit market-calendar and `as_of`
   semantics, an idempotency key, advisory lock, durable TaskRun, transactional outbox event, and
   replay path.
2. A retry or concurrent invocation of the same logical Daily job has exactly one business effect;
   failures are bounded, sanitized, durable, and visible.
3. Reports distinguish facts, deterministic calculations, Agent judgments, assumptions, and human
   decisions, and never present a recommendation as an executed trade.
4. All four UI pages are usable with synthetic/de-identified data and prominently retain the V1
   simulation/no-auto-trade, freshness, Veto, approval, and exception signals.
5. No scheduler trigger, API/UI action, or report path can bypass Risk Veto, valid human approval,
   immutable history, or disabled live execution.

## Out of scope and human gates

Real brokerage execution, credentials, real Portfolio data, real investment-policy choices, and
Learning proposal activation remain forbidden. A real exchange calendar/data-provider authorization
or a change to investment governance requires explicit human approval.
