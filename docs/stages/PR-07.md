## Objective

Deliver the auditable Decision path with explicit CIO gates, immutable human approval actions,
paper/manual execution records, and a complete Decision Journal. All work uses synthetic or
authorized de-identified data. V1 retains `auto_trade=false`: live brokerage orders and
credentials are forbidden.

## Deliverables

- [x] CIO aggregation that preserves Thesis/investment quality, timing, portfolio fit, risk, and
  policy/data sufficiency as explicit gates rather than a composite score
- [ ] Immutable Decision aggregate, state transitions, persistence, and typed API
- [x] Immutable human `APPROVE`/`REJECT`/`REVOKE` records with fail-closed approval TTL semantics
- [x] `DisabledLiveExecutionAdapter` that categorically rejects typed live-order submission
- [x] Immutable paper/manual execution records linked to valid human approval
- [ ] Complete Decision Journal, audit trail, and synthetic end-to-end S10 coverage
- [ ] Stage evidence and project-status update

## Acceptance criteria

1. CIO aggregation cannot turn weak investment quality into BUY/ADD merely from timing, or turn
   strong timing into BUY/ADD where the Thesis, portfolio, Risk, Policy, or data gate fails.
2. Every Decision preserves the exact evidence, Thesis/Policy/Strategy/Prompt and sizing versions,
   risk result, Core/Tactical actions, unknowns, dissent, reasons, and review schedule used at the
   decision time.
3. A named human action is append-only. Only a current `APPROVE` within the Policy TTL can move a
   Decision forward; rejection, revocation, expiry, or material input/price drift cannot be bypassed.
4. Risk Veto continues to block BUY/ADD and increased exposure; CIO and approval cannot override it.
   REDUCE/EXIT retains its separately recorded risk-reducing path.
5. No live order can be submitted in V1. The only live adapter rejects every request; paper and
   manual records remain distinct, immutable execution records linked to a valid approval.
6. Synthetic S10 end-to-end coverage reconstructs the audit chain from Evidence through Decision,
   human action, and paper/manual execution. Normal, failure, and boundary tests cover every new
   behavior.

## Human decisions and out of scope

The owner must choose any real approval actor, TTL, price-drift tolerance, Portfolio data, and
investment-policy limits before those can be used outside synthetic tests. A broker integration,
credentials, live execution, or a change to `auto_trade=false` requires a new/superseding ADR and
explicit human authorization; none is in scope for this stage.
